# Data deletion

This page explains how to have your data deleted from Bodhinox Chat. The [Privacy policy](/privacy) explains what we keep and for how long.

## If you have a Bodhinox Chat account

- **Delete a workspace:** the workspace owner opens **Settings → Privacy & access** and chooses to delete the workspace. Sending stops at once. The workspace can be restored for 30 days in case the request was a mistake; after that its contacts, conversations, media, campaigns, orders and settings are erased, except billing and tax records the law requires us to keep.
- **Delete your account:** open **Settings → Privacy & access** and delete your account. Your sign-in identity is anonymised and your sessions and API keys are revoked at once. Workspaces where you are the only member are closed as above. Workspaces you shared with others stay with their owners.
- **Export first:** you can download a copy of a workspace from the same page before deleting it.
- **Disconnect an account you connected:** disconnecting a WhatsApp number, Instagram account, Facebook Page or other integration in Settings stops new data arriving from it; disconnecting a WhatsApp number also deletes our access token for it.

## If you connected a Facebook account

Bodhinox Chat uses Facebook Login only to let a business connect its WhatsApp Business account, Instagram account, Facebook Pages or ad accounts to a workspace. We do not store your Facebook profile, friends, posts or other personal Facebook data.

To remove our access, go to your Facebook **Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations** (or **Apps and websites**), select Bodhinox Chat and remove it. Facebook then withdraws our access and sends us a deletion request; you get a confirmation code and a link to this page where you can check its status. To delete business data that a workspace holds (messages, contacts, orders), the workspace owner deletes it as described above.

## If you messaged a business that uses Bodhinox Chat

Your messages and contact details belong to that business's workspace, and the business decides what happens to them. Ask the business to delete your data; reply STOP on WhatsApp to stop its campaigns. If you cannot reach the business, contact us with the business's name and your phone number. We will pass the request on and help where we can, after confirming that the number is yours.

## Contact

For any other deletion request, write to the address on our [Contact page](/contact). We acknowledge requests within 3 working days and complete verified requests within 30 days.
