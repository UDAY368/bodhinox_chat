# Privacy policy

**DRAFT. This text is a placeholder and is not in force. The operator of this service must replace it with a policy reviewed by a lawyer, including retention periods and the operator's contact details, before relying on it.**

## 1. Who we are

To be completed by the operator: legal name, address and contact for privacy questions.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account data:** your name, email address, password (stored only as a hash), two-step verification settings and sign-in records.
- **Workspace data:** the contacts, conversations, messages, orders, appointments and other records you and your customers create. You decide what goes in; we process it on your behalf.
- **Billing data:** your plan, invoices and GST details. Card and bank details are handled by the payment provider, not stored by us.
- **Technical data:** logs needed to run and secure the service. We do not put message content or secrets in logs.

## 3. How we use it

To provide the service, keep it secure, bill for it, and support you when you ask. We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Who can see it

Members of your workspace, according to their role. Support staff only with access you approve, for a limited time and without message content or contact details. Service providers that host the service, send email and process payments.

## 5. Your choices

You can export your workspace data, delete your workspace, leave a workspace and delete your account from **Settings → Privacy & access**.

## 6. Retention

To be completed by the operator. Deleted workspaces are kept for at least 30 days so a request can be cancelled; billing and tax records are kept as the law requires.

## 7. Changes

We will tell you about material changes to this policy.
