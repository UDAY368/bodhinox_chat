# Privacy, data and your account

Open **Settings → Privacy & access** for everything on this page.

## Export your workspace data

Owners and admins can export the workspace's own data: contacts, notes, custom fields, tags, lists, segments, conversations, messages, pipelines and deals, flows, automations, templates, quick replies, products, orders, appointments and invoices. The export is a zip file with one JSON Lines file per kind of data and a manifest.

- If you use two-step verification, you confirm your authenticator code first.
- The file is prepared in the background. Download it from the same page; the download link works for 7 days, then the file is removed.
- Passwords, access tokens, keys, encrypted values and other credentials are never included.
- Only one export can be in progress at a time. Every request and download is recorded in the audit log.

## Support access

Support staff cannot see your workspace on their own. When they need to look at technical details (for example, the health of an integration or failed background jobs), they ask for access with a reason and a time limit of at most 24 hours. Owners and admins approve, deny or revoke the request from this page, and every step is recorded. Support access never includes message content or contact details.

## Delete the workspace

The owner can ask for the workspace to be deleted. You confirm with your password (and your authenticator code if you use two-step verification).

- Sending stops immediately: campaigns, flows, automations and AI replies are paused. Incoming messages and opt-outs keep working.
- A paid plan is cancelled at the end of the current period.
- Nothing is deleted during the 30-day grace period, and the owner can cancel the request at any time during it. Export your data first if you want to keep a copy.

## Leave a workspace

Admins, agents and viewers can leave a workspace they no longer work in. You are moved to your other workspace. The owner must transfer ownership before leaving. If it is your only workspace, delete your account instead.

## Create another workspace

Anyone can create another workspace that they own, for example for a second business. You switch to it straight away.

## Delete your account

Deleting your account signs you out everywhere and removes your name, email address, password and two-step verification. You cannot delete your account while you own a workspace that has other members: transfer ownership first. Workspaces where you are the only member get a deletion request (see above). Records of what you did in a workspace keep an anonymous reference so the workspace's history stays complete.
