# Plans, limits and billing

Your workspace plan decides which features are available and how much of each limit you can use. Open **Settings → Plan & usage** to see the plan, its features and the usage meters.

## Prices

Plan prices are shown on the pricing page and in **Settings → Plan & usage → Billing** when online billing is available. Amounts are **inclusive of GST**: the amount charged is the invoice total, and the GST in it is shown on each invoice.

## What happens at a limit

- **Hard limits** (team members, segments, lists, active flows, active automations, WhatsApp numbers, Instagram accounts, appointment staff, integrations, storage): new additions are refused once the limit is reached. Nothing you already have is removed.
- **Contacts**: contacts you add by hand, import or create through the API count against the limit. Contacts created because a customer wrote to you are always accepted, even over the limit.
- **AI replies and AI tokens**: refused before the AI is called once the monthly allowance is used. Manual replies by your team are never affected.
- **Monthly active contacts and messages this month**: shown and warned about, never blocked.

Owners and admins get a notification at 80% and at 100% of a limit, once per limit and month.

## Changing, cancelling and unpaid plans

- When a paid plan ends or is cancelled, the workspace moves to the free plan at the end of the paid period. Items that no longer fit (segments, lists, flows, automations) are archived, never deleted, and an owner chooses what to keep.
- When a payment fails, there is a grace period. After it, sending is paused until the plan is paid. Incoming messages, opt-outs and delivery updates keep working.
